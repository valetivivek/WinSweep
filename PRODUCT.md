# WinSweep: Product context

register: product

## Product purpose

A Windows desktop app for managing installed software: see what is installed,
keep it updated through winget, and clean up the files left behind after
uninstalls. The job is control and confidence over your own machine.

## Users

People who actually look after their PC: developers, power users, tinkerers,
the friend everyone asks to "fix the laptop." They are comfortable with paths,
versions, and registry keys. They distrust utilities that hide what they are
doing or nag with fake urgency. They want speed, precision, and a tool that
respects that destructive actions are destructive.

## Brand and tone

Terminal-native and confident. Precise, calm, quietly technical. WinSweep
speaks like a good CLI with a great front end: it shows the real path, the real
size, the real version, and never dramatizes. Trust is the whole product, so it
is most careful exactly where it is most powerful (deletion, uninstall).

The visual identity is monospace-forward (JetBrains Mono), with a single
committed electric-iris accent, crisp dividers, and tabular figures. It should
read like an instrument, not a wizard.

## Anti-references

- CCleaner / generic registry-cleaner scareware: fake "847 issues found!"
  urgency, big red FIX buttons, hero "GB to reclaim" gauges.
- Windows Settings blandness: gray, characterless, no point of view.
- SaaS dashboard cliche: identical card grids, icon-heading-text tiles,
  pastel gradients, a hero metric on every screen.
- Consumer-cute: rounded blobby mascots, playful color, emoji.

## Strategic principles

- Show the truth plainly. Real paths, real sizes, real versions. No invented urgency.
- Destructive actions are guarded, never automatic. Confirmation, clear stakes, nothing pre-selected for deletion.
- The tool disappears into the task. Density and speed over decoration.
- One coherent motif everywhere: same radius, dividers, hover, spacing, type vocabulary.
- Light mode is the default; dark mode is first-class, not an afterthought.

Principles

Truth first

Real paths, versions, sources, sizes, and limitations stay visible.

One accent

Electric iris carries actions, selection, focus, and active state.

No scareware

No fake urgency, giant danger claims, or theatrical cleanup metrics.

Windows native

The site supports the desktop app instead of hiding its platform reality.

Surfaces

Major panes use a double-bezel feel: a soft outer shell, an inner highlight, and restrained radius. It should read like a clean instrument panel rather than a toy dashboard.

Motion

Motion is intentionally quiet. Sections reveal with weight, controls press down, and preview tabs update without page navigation. The site respects reduced motion.

Content

Copy avoids vague software promises. It states what WinSweep does today, calls out development status, and keeps destructive actions framed around review and consent.
